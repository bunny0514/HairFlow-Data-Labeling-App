Section 1

Overview & Goal

You're helping build a training dataset for a model that understands hair appearance. Our priority is to label what is visible in each image. If you are unsure, choose "Unsure/Not Visible" rather than guessing. Consistency beats precision.

Primary goal:
  • Classify hair characteristics (curl pattern, length, density, color, style, etc.).
  • Mark image conditions (lighting, occlusion, blur) that affect visibility.
Important:
  • Base labels on the current photo only. Do not infer from context.
  • Use Unsure/Not Visible when the view is obstructed or out of frame.

Section 3

Labeling Protocol (step‑by‑step)

  1. Scan the image for lighting, occlusion, and whether the whole hair area is visible.
  2. Assign curl pattern using the table above. If styled/altered, choose the visible pattern and note the style in 2.6.
  3. Pick length based on what you can see. If ends are hidden, choose Unsure/Not Visible.
  4. Estimate density by scalp visibility at the part/crown.
  5. Estimate strand thickness, or choose Unsure if image resolution is low.
  6. Select color(s) and style. Add protective style/wig if applicable.
  7. Flag image conditions (lighting/blur/occlusion/face status).
  8. Final check: If you guessed on any field, switch to Unsure. Consistency wins.
Section 4

Quality Rules & Edge Cases

Label ✅
  • Clear view of hair with acceptable lighting.
  • Protective styles (braids, twists, locs) — label the style and set pattern to Unsure if natural pattern is hidden.
  • Wigs/weaves/extensions — mark style as such; label visible attributes.
Do NOT label ❌
  • Images with explicit content, watermarks, or personal identifiers that violate consent.
  • Children without clear guardian consent documentation (if present in your queue).
  • Severely blurred/obscured hair where core attributes are not visible.

Section 6

Privacy, Safety & Consent

If an image appears to be used without consent or includes sensitive personal data, stop and flag it immediately. Our dataset requires that images are permissibly collected and used. Faces should be blurred when possible; if a face is clearly identifiable and cannot be blurred in the labeling tool, mark Face visible and escalate.

  • Never download or share images outside the labeling tool.
  • Do not try to identify the person; label hair only.
  • If you suspect a child’s image lacks consent, do not label and flag.
Section 7

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I can’t tell between 3C and 4A?

Choose the closest visible type; if still unsure, pick Unsure. Consistency matters more than perfect granularity. Use notes if the tool allows.

How do I label wigs or extensions?

Mark Wig or Weave/Extensions in Style. Label visible attributes (length/color) as seen. Natural pattern can be Unsure if hidden.

Should I correct obvious photo filters?

No. Label what you see and flag Image Conditions if color seems altered.

Do you want segmentation/boxes?

This guide covers classification labels. If your task includes drawing masks or boxes, follow the separate Annotation UI guide provided in the tool.
